Credit Card Customer & Transaction Analysis | Power BI

Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing credit card customer data and transaction performance using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard provides insights into revenue, transaction amounts, customer demographics, spending behaviour, card categories, and customer satisfaction.

Objectives

Analyze overall credit card revenue and transaction performance.

Understand customer demographics and spending patterns.

Identify high-revenue customer segments.

Analyze revenue by card category, payment method, and expenditure type.

Track quarterly revenue and transaction trends.

Support data-driven business decisions through interactive dashboards.

Tools & Technologies

Microsoft Power BI

Power Query

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

Microsoft Excel / CSV

Data Visualization

Dashboards

1. Credit Card Transaction Report

This dashboard analyzes credit card transaction performance.

Key KPIs:

Total Revenue

Total Transaction Amount

Total Interest Earned

Total Transactions

Analysis includes:

Revenue by card category

Revenue by expenditure type

Revenue by payment method

Revenue by customer job

Revenue by education level

Quarterly revenue and transaction analysis

2. Credit Card Customer Report

This dashboard analyzes customer demographics and behaviour.

Analysis includes:

Revenue by gender

Revenue by age group

Revenue by marital status

Revenue by income group

Revenue by customer job

Revenue by state

Customer satisfaction analysis

Revenue by education level

Key Business Insights

Blue card category contributes the highest revenue.

Swipe transactions generate a significant share of revenue.

Bills are among the leading expenditure categories.

Businessmen represent a major revenue-generating customer segment.

Customers in the 40–50 age group contribute significantly to revenue.

High-income customers contribute a substantial share of total revenue.

How to Use

Download the .pbix Power BI file.

Refresh the data if the original dataset is available.

Explore the dashboards using interactive filters and slicers.

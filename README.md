# Credit Card Customer & Transaction Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview
A Power BI project analyzing credit card customer data and transaction performance, with insights into revenue, transaction amounts, demographics, spending behaviour, card categories, and customer satisfaction.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall revenue and transaction performance
- Understand customer demographics and spending patterns
- Identify high-revenue customer segments
- Break down revenue by card category, payment method, and expenditure type
- Track quarterly revenue and transaction trends
- Support data-driven decisions through interactive dashboards

## Tools & Technologies
Power BI · Power Query · DAX · Excel / CSV · Data Visualization

## Dashboards

### 1. Credit Card Transaction Report
**KPIs:** Total Revenue · Total Transaction Amount · Total Interest Earned · Total Transactions

**Analysis:** revenue by card category, expenditure type, payment method, customer job, and education level; quarterly revenue and transaction trends.

### 2. Credit Card Customer Report
**Analysis:** revenue by gender, age group, marital status, income group, customer job, state, and education level; customer satisfaction.

## Key Business Insights
- The Blue card category contributes the highest revenue.
- Swipe transactions generate a significant share of revenue.
- Bills are among the leading expenditure categories.
- Businessmen are a major revenue-generating segment.
- Customers aged 40–50 contribute significantly to revenue.
- High-income customers account for a substantial share of revenue.

## Interactive Features
Quarter-wise, gender, and credit score filters · customer segmentation · interactive charts and KPI cards

## How to Use
1. Download the `.pbix` file.
2. Open it in Power BI Desktop.
3. Refresh the data if the original dataset is available.
4. Explore using the filters and slicers.
